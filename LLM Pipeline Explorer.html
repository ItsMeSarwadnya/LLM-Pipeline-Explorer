STEP 04

Positional Encoding — Word Order & Waves

Sinusoidal PE · 16 Dims

Transformers process all tokens in parallel, so they have no built-in sense of word order. Sinusoidal positional encodings inject unique sine and cosine wave signatures into every token position so the model knows where each token sits.
